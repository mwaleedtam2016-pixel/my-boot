Key Architecture

Built For Seamless Administration

Enterprise stability, zero database corruption, and full admin autonomy.

💬

Dual Command Execution

Whether your members prefer modern Discord Slash Commands (/salary, /invest) or lightning-fast natural Arabic text keywords (راتب, استثمار), Fire Bank supports both flawlessly.

⏱️

Precise Cooldown Engine

Every single economic command is safeguarded by an intelligent cooldown tracker, completely preventing spam macros, automated bot exploitation, and database flooding.

🔒

Channel Whitelisting

Keep your general chat clean. Server administrators can bind Fire Bank commands to designated channels with /abb-channel, ensuring spam-free discussions.

⚡

Zero-Latency Transactions

In-memory transaction processing backed by atomic disk persistence guarantees immediate feedback on coin transfers, bets, and stock orders without race conditions.

🌐

Bilingual Native Support

Designed from the ground up with full Arabic localization (Right-to-Left formatting, elegant currency symbols, and tailored terminology) along with English command parity.

🛡️

Discord Policy Compliant

100% compliant with Discord Developer Terms of Service, GDPR privacy rights, and strict virtual-only entertainment guidelines. Zero real-money gambling.
